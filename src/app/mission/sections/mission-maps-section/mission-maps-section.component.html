Geocoding

Google geocoding API key — optional Stored only in this browser, and billed to your own Google account.

Neither option works offline. Address lookup defaults to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — free, no key. Supplying a Google key switches lookup to Google Geocoding, which is generally more accurate for addresses OpenStreetMap covers less well.

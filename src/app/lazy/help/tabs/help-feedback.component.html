Contact us

RangerTrak is a free, open-source project from SteadyEOC (rangertrak.com). There are two ways to reach us - pick the one that fits what you want to say:

  • In public: the feedback form below, or the project on GitHub. It becomes a GitHub issue that anyone can read, and other users can add to. Best for a problem or an idea.
  • In private: email RangerTrak@steadyeoc.com. Only the maintainer reads it. Best for anything with names, places or mission details, or that you'd rather not post.

Not all feedback needs to become an issue - if you're unsure, describe what you saw and let the maintainer sort it.

Feedback or an AAR (after-action review) note? Feedback goes to RangerTrak's developers right away, as a public GitHub issue, and needs a connection. An AAR note (the button at the top of every page) stays private on this device for your own team's after-action review. It works offline and is part of the mission backup. In the field with no signal, or no time? Press AAR note and mark it RangerTrak. Later, Review and send on the AAR notes page brings those notes here, for you to check before anything is sent.

RangerTrak is free and will stay free. If it's useful to your team, you can support its development at buymeacoffee.com/JohnCornelison. That goes to the developer personally and isn't tax-deductible. Feedback helps just as much.

RangerTrak keeps a running record of what it did this session - useful when something goes wrong and you want to report it, or figure out why.

Open the Log page

The easiest way to include it is the Include a diagnostic report box on the feedback form above: press Preview to see exactly what would be added. It holds your version, browser and storage details and a short list of what the app did, and leaves out ranger names, report text and places. To keep a copy for yourself, press Copy diagnostics. For the full record, press Copy log to clipboard on the Log page and paste it into a private email rather than the public form.

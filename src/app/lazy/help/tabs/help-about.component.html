What RangerTrak is

A log of who is where, when, and in what condition, for teams reachable only by voice radio. Field teams radio in; you transcribe; RangerTrak maps it and keeps the record.

It runs entirely in this browser, on this device. No server, no account, no login. Your mission stays on this device and is sent nowhere unless you choose to, for example by looking up an address.

Two phases, even when one person does both

Setup is infrequent and considered — Mission and Rangers, before or between operational periods. Entry is repetitive and time-critical — the entry screen, while reports come in. The app is built around the second one being fast.

RangerTrak is a free, open-source project from SteadyEOC. To report a problem or suggest something, see the Feedback tab.

  • A Progressive Web App: installable, and runs with no Internet once loaded
  • Angular 22 with TypeScript, signals, zoneless change detection, and Material Design
  • Leaflet maps with marker clustering, an overview map, and offline tile saving
  • MapLibre GL + PMTiles: a vector map with basemap data bundled into the app
  • Address lookup via OpenStreetMap's Nominatim by default, with no API key - an optional Google Geocoding key can be supplied instead on the Mission page's Maps card
  • Plus Codes and all lat/long conversion computed on-device
  • AG Grid tables with searching, sorting, filtering and export
  • No cookies, no accounts, no server: data stays in your browser's local storage

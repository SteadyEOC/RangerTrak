Starting a device over

Delete the reports on the Radio Log page and the roster on the Rangers page, then reset settings under Mission → Danger zone. Back up anything you need first — none of it can be undone.

Load sample mission, Restore mission and Reset mission to defaults all replace what is on this device. Back up first if you need to keep it. See Your data for what a mission's data actually is and where it lives.

After-action notes

During the mission, AAR note (AAR: after-action review) at the top of every page records something to fix or improve later, without leaving the page you are on. Each note is marked as about this incident or about RangerTrak.

At the debrief, open AAR notes. Give each note an area, a recommendation and an owner. Print produces an improvement-plan table of the incident notes, and Export downloads every note as a spreadsheet. Notes about RangerTrak can go to its developers through Review and send, which opens the Feedback form so you can check them first.

Notes are part of Back up mission. Deleting the radio log leaves them alone; delete them on the AAR notes page when you no longer need them.

AAR notes and Feedback are different things. An AAR note stays private on this device, for your own team's debrief. Feedback (Help → Feedback) goes straight to RangerTrak's developers as a public GitHub issue - only a note marked RangerTrak can be passed to it, through Review and send, and only after you check it there.

What to keep, what to clear

After the mission Do this
Keep a record of what happened Back up mission&ngsp;(Mission → Data safety) before clearing anything — settings, roster and reports in one file
Clear reports for the next mission Radio Log → Delete all radio log entries
Reuse the same roster next time Leave the roster alone — it is not tied to a single mission and does not need to be re-imported
Start completely fresh Mission → Danger zone → Reset mission to defaults, plus clearing the roster and reports above

The roster holds participant personal data. If this device is being handed off, reused for another purpose, or is not yours, clear the roster too — see Your data for why it is confidential.

Uninstalling RangerTrak

Uninstalling the app doesn't always delete its data. On iPhone and iPad, removing it does. On a computer, Chrome and Edge ask you first. On Android it varies by device. To be sure, back up what you need, then clear the site data for rangertrak.org before uninstalling. See the "Deleting your data" section of RangerTrak's privacy policy for detail.

Starting a device over

Back up first: none of this can be undone. Loading a demo, Start a real mission and Delete radio log entries and messages each show a What to clear list before they do anything:

  • A demo or a real mission replaces the whole mission. The roster, ranger photos, radio log (with its attachments), places and settings always go. Notes about this mission, ICS-214 lines and the ICS-214 header start ticked; untick any you want to keep. Notes about RangerTrak start unticked.
  • Deleting the radio log clears only the log and its attachments. Everything else starts unticked.
  • The list has Back up mission first. During a live mission you also type the mission name before it goes ahead.

For a full reset, also use Delete all rangers on the Rangers page and Reset mission to defaults under Mission → Danger zone.

Load sample mission, Restore mission and Reset mission to defaults all replace what is on this device. Back up first if you need to keep it. See Your data for what a mission's data actually is and where it lives.

After-action notes

During the mission, AAR/214 (AAR: after-action review) at the top of every page records something to fix or improve later, without leaving the page you are on. At the top of the box, choose Mission for a note about this mission or RangerTrak for one about the app itself.

The same button adds a line to your ICS-214 activity log: what you did or what changed at your position, such as arriving, a shift change or a radio that stopped working. A 214 line is not a radio call, so it does not go in the radio log. Choose ICS-214 at the top of the box; the time is now unless you change it, which is handy when you write things down late. On the AAR notes page, the ICS-214 section lists the lines, and Export ICS-214 fills in FEMA's own ICS-214 form from them, ready to print and sign.

At the debrief, open AAR notes. Give each note an area, a recommendation and an owner. Print produces an improvement-plan table of the incident notes, and Export downloads every note as a spreadsheet. Notes about RangerTrak can go to its developers through Review and send, which opens the Feedback form so you can check them first.

Mission summary

Current summary, on the AAR notes page and the Radio Log page, shows where the mission stands: how many messages came in each hour, which teams, stations and operators they came from, the entries that need attention, and your AAR notes grouped by area. Choose this operational period, the whole log, or dates of your own. You can run it at any time: at the end of a period, at the end of the mission, or just to see how things are going. It only reads the radio log, so it never stops logging and nothing is closed or locked. Running it later simply gives a newer summary.

Print and Export make the record of that moment. Each is stamped with the mission, the period it covers, and when it was made, so bring the printout to the after-action review. Nothing is saved on the device, so print or export it if you want to keep it.

Notes are part of Back up mission. Deleting the radio log leaves them alone; delete them on the AAR notes page when you no longer need them.

AAR notes and Feedback are different things. An AAR note stays private on this device, for your own team's debrief. Feedback (Help → Contact us) goes straight to RangerTrak's developers as a public GitHub issue - only a note marked RangerTrak can be passed to it, through Review and send, and only after you check it there.

What to keep, what to clear

After the mission Do this
Keep a record of what happened Back up mission&ngsp;(Mission → Data safety) before clearing anything — settings, roster and reports in one file
Clear reports for the next mission Radio Log → Delete radio log entries and messages; in its What to clear list, tick notes or 214 lines only if they should go too
Reuse the same roster next time Leave the roster alone — it is not tied to a single mission and does not need to be re-imported
Start completely fresh Start a real mission on Entry clears the roster, log, places and settings in one step; or clear them one at a time as above

The roster holds participant personal data. If this device is being handed off, reused for another purpose, or is not yours, clear the roster too — see Your data for why it is confidential.

Uninstalling RangerTrak

Uninstalling the app doesn't always delete its data. On iPhone and iPad, removing it does. On a computer, Chrome and Edge ask you first. On Android it varies by device. To be sure, back up what you need, then clear the site data for rangertrak.org before uninstalling. See the "Deleting your data" section of RangerTrak's privacy policy for detail.
